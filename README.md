# Critical and Massive Machine-Type Communication towards 6G

Group white paper written for the Mobile Communication Systems course
at Aalto University (2025). It is a literature-based course report, not a
peer-reviewed publication, and it is mostly based on the University of
Oulu 6G white paper on critical and massive MTC.

## Contents
Drivers, use cases and KPIs for MTC towards 6G; enabling technologies
(cell-free architecture, grant-free access, energy-efficient devices,
mission-critical MTC); privacy and security; sustainability.

## My contribution
Privacy and security section (group-based and physical-layer
authentication, distributed ledger technology, physical unclonable
functions).

## Authors
Nine students; see the paper for the full author list.
